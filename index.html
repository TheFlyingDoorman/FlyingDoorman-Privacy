Flying Doorman Travel

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

Flying Doorman Travel ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your data when using the app.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information. When you register, we collect your email address and a password (stored in encrypted form) to create and secure your account.
  • Content you create. Cities, entries, notes, addresses, categories, and any other content you add to the catalogue are stored so you can view and edit them across your devices.
  • Technical data. Standard log data (such as device type, operating system, and time-stamps of requests) may be collected by our backend for reliability, security, and troubleshooting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To store, sync, and display your travel entries across your devices.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the reliability of the app.
  • To communicate service-related messages, such as verification codes or invitations you request.

3. Sharing and Visibility of Content

Flying Doorman Travel is a shared travel catalogue. Cities and entries you add are visible to other authenticated users of the app so that the group can share recommendations. Only you (as the author) or an administrator can edit or delete an entry you created. Your email address and password are never shown to other users.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Supabase (an open-source backend service) for authentication, database storage, and email delivery of verification codes and invitations. Data transmitted to these services is encrypted in transit. We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Data Retention

Your account and content are retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Backend log data may be retained for a limited period for security and diagnostic purposes.

6. Your Rights

  • Access, update, or correct your account information from within the app.
  • Delete individual entries or cities you have created at any time.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated content by emailing us.

7. Children's Privacy

Flying Doorman Travel is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, and network requests use HTTPS/TLS encryption. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective Date" above. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to exercise your rights, please contact us: contact@onspace.ai
