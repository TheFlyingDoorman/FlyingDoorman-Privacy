What to Include

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include the following details in your email:

  • Device and OS version (e.g., iPhone 15, iOS 18)
  • App version (found in the App Store listing)
  • A description of the issue and the steps to reproduce it
  • Screenshots if applicable

Common Questions

  • How do I create an account? Open the app and enter your email on the sign-in screen. You'll receive a one-time verification code by email to complete sign-up.
  • Can I use the app on multiple devices? Yes. Sign in with the same email on each device and your cities and entries will sync automatically.
  • How do I delete an entry or city I created? Open the entry or city and tap the delete icon. Only the original author or an administrator can delete an item.
  • Can I request an account deletion? Absolutely — email us at the address above and we'll remove your account and content.

Response Time

We typically respond to support requests within 2-3 business days. Thanks in advance for your patience.
